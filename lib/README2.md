Product Owner / Manager Case Study – E-ticketing system

Context

For this use-case study purposes, you’ll be acting as the only key-person and as a mid-
level Product Manager responsible for an online e-ticket management system used for

purchasing events, courses, sports, venue and concert tickets.
our vision will be the platform who has the easiest and fastest and cheapest for partners to
connect with and we don’t have our mission set straight at the moment.
The platform operates in UAE, Singapore and India, supporting high-traffic ticket sales,
online and offline digital payments, seat and reservation allocation, advance ticket
issuance, reporting, etc. Current payment methods we are accepting are: Visa/Mastercard
CNP/CP and UPI.
You work as an individual contributor, closely with Engineering, Operations, and Security
teams. All production issues, customer complaints, and system alerts are tracked through
a project management platform. Well structured company that employs 20-30 employees
on Product, Business, Tech alone.
Our technology stacks revolve arounds
1. ReactJS, JQuery, Flutter for mobile app
2. Golang, NodeJS, and some legacy services written in old .net and java
3. Amazon EC2 Kubernetes (With some legacy services running scattered)
4. OracleDB (for speed, supports and super costly)
Tasks
For this use-case study purposes, we want to have the Product Manager to give us
1. Short page or slide describing product assessment and a roadmap on how 2026
landscape and how we pivot our product.
2. Demonstrate on the end-to-end process handling a long term issue that keeps
recurring every time ( described below).
3. Demonstrate on how we make feature assessment, plan and execution and which
methodology and process we’re doing this feature assessment from just an idea to
deployment.

Long term issue
The success rate of payments made on our e-ticketing system are less than < 60%, with
some known errors are due to
• Payment declined (8%)
• No payments inputted (20%)
• Other error types (Total 12%)
o System error (6%)
o Timeout (4%)
o Anomaly (2%)

Explain how you assess, prioritize, and handle these long term issues. Describe your role as
a PM and how you communicate decisions to technical and non-technical stakeholders.

Feature development requirement and process
Business and management have an idea of having a new idea of enabling PayPal payment
channels thru our system, while at the same time they thought it may resolve our payment
error rates issue. But business may not be aware of how the visual and the integration will
look like and how the success should be measured.
Explain how a role of product manager assesses this requirement, describe what kinds of
documents required, and what are there to provide by what departments and how we run
this process end-to-end.
